Repair

Use this if the backup and the NAS have fallen out of step — after a crash or an interrupted run, or on a new PC. It rebuilds the local index from the NAS and re-creates any missing index entries from the pack files themselves. It never deletes anything unless you ask it to.

Registers a Windows Task Scheduler entry. The phone must be reachable when it fires — plugged in, or paired over Wi-Fi.
